4Research Journey

What started as pure curiosity in linear algebra, stochastic optimization, and deep neural architectures became the foundation of everything we build today. The curiosity never stops there.

The United Kingdom — London and Basingstoke — is where we grounded our clinical work. High-precision sensor ML for smart prosthetic mobility shaped our standards for mission-critical engineering.

But good engineering doesn't stay in one place — and neither did we. We go wherever the next technical frontier takes us.

From volumetric 3D medical vision to multimodal generative AI and distributed global systems — the people and the problems give us reasons to keep going.
